Research date: 17 October 2026. Supports [Assess current compiler ecosystems and career relevance](https://github.com/awjreynolds/p4ai/issues/7). This is a provisional recommendation for target selection, not the final target decision. Sources are project documentation, vendor documentation, and employer listings; no tools or workloads were executed.

Focused follow-up evidence is tracked separately in [Assess a current spatial NPU compiler target](https://github.com/awjreynolds/p4ai/issues/8) and [Assess a current NVIDIA GPU compiler target](https://github.com/awjreynolds/p4ai/issues/9). ## Recommendation

**Prioritise a bounded NVIDIA-aligned GPU/AI compiler experiment**, reflecting the user's interest in NVIDIA and proximity to Bristol. Investigate Triton and IREE's NVIDIA backend before choosing the compiler boundary; the IREE constraint evidence alone does not establish equivalent NVIDIA coverage. eBPF remains the strongest networking-adjacent alternative, and AMD MLIR-AIE/IRON a follow-up candidate. Exclude Tofino as requested. P4 experience provides transferable constrained-hardware compiler skills, without determining the new target.

This ranking is an inference from the evidence below. It combines the user's public reproducibility requirement with the desire to demonstrate skills relevant to active compiler development. It does not rank salaries, count vacancies, or forecast employment. “P4”, “MLIR”, and “GPU” also describe different layers: the experiment ultimately needs a named compiler boundary, hardware architecture, and independently enforced constraints.

## GPU and AI compilers: the clearest compiler career signal

IREE currently documents compiler-generated tuning constraints, including workgroup limits, shared memory limits, intrinsic choices, and shape restrictions. Its tuner converts those constraints to SMT-LIB and enumerates legal assignments. This is unusually close to the proposed mechanical → Typesafe Jev → high-reasoning escalation structure: there is an existing deterministic legality layer that must remain authoritative. The research challenge becomes whether escalation improves decisions under a fixed budget beyond that strong baseline. [IREE tuning](https://iree.dev/reference/tuning/)

The surrounding ecosystem offers public contribution routes. IREE lists AMD-SHARK projects and its PyTorch frontend, while its governance documentation describes open technical steering meetings, code review, and a path from contributor to code owner. It asks contributors to coordinate before substantial work. A useful downstream experiment could therefore expose a small upstream opportunity in diagnostics, constraint coverage, reproducible failures, or tuning measurement. Upstream acceptance remains a separate decision by maintainers. [IREE community](https://iree.dev/community/), [contributing](https://iree.dev/developers/general/contributing/)

Triton's current repository offers source builds, compiler IR inspection, reproduction facilities and tests without a GPU, while full tests require one. Its advertised supported hardware includes NVIDIA and AMD GPUs. The same repository advertises a developer conference for 19 October 2026, evidence of an active technical community. These are concrete entry points for contribution and visibility, rather than proof that a particular optimisation project is novel. [Triton repository](https://github.com/triton-lang/triton/blob/main/README.md)

The strongest geographically relevant employer evidence is NVIDIA's official **Compiler Engineer – AI Inference**, JR2023372, listing **UK Remote** and **UK Cambridge**. It describes GPU kernel generation, computational graph optimisation and hardware/software co-design, and requests experience with compiler optimisations, synthesis, placement and MLIR. This matches the user's existing skills and proposed direction substantially better than a generic global AI vacancy. It is not a Bristol office role. ## eBPF: a strong bridge from networking

Cilium's documentation places eBPF in networking, tracing and security, and describes its use in Cilium's datapath. Its Go eBPF library has a first major 2026 feature release. This is current infrastructure with real operational consumers, not merely a language proposal. [Cilium BPF guide](https://docs.cilium.io/en/stable/bpf/index.html), [ebpf-go releases](https://github.com/cilium/ebpf/releases)

The eBPF Foundation's governing board includes representatives from CrowdStrike, Google, Isovalent, Meta, Netflix and Toyota. That demonstrates organisational participation, not a hiring census. A 2026 eBPF community case study documents Cloudflare's move toward programmable networking and away from hardware dependency. Together these sources support eBPF as a plausible direction for networking expertise. [Foundation board](https://ebpf.foundation/board/), [Cloudflare case study](https://ebpf.io/blog/cloudflare-replatforming-1/)

The career inference differs from the GPU recommendation: eBPF work can demonstrate verifier-aware program construction, kernel knowledge, security and datapath engineering. It is attractive if the desired future role remains close to networking. It is less direct evidence of spatial compiler scheduling unless the experiment explicitly chooses a compiler optimisation problem. A system that merely edits code until a verifier accepts it risks demonstrating repair automation more than target-resource compilation.

## P4, SmartNICs and FPGA: separate community vitality from access

P4 remains active. Its 12 October 2026 workshop focused on AI-native networks and included heterogeneous SmartNIC compilation; the programme committee included people from Google, Cisco and Xsight Labs. That supports continued technical relevance and the usefulness of existing contacts. It does not prove a growing P4 compiler employment market or an openly reproducible current ASIC target. [2026 P4 Workshop](https://p4.org/2026-workshop/)

Likewise, NVIDIA publishes a July 2026 DOCA 3.5.0 release and describes BlueField-4 work. Current DPU investment must not be equated with investment in every associated programming language, or with a public backend suitable for this experiment. A contact-assisted path is worth retaining only if it produces a specific current target whose compiler and constraints can be used and reported publicly. [DOCA releases](https://developer.nvidia.com/doca-archive), [DOCA platform](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/networking/products/software/doca/)

AMD's Vitis 2026.1 release demonstrates continuing FPGA and AI Engine compiler development. Its licensing page distinguishes no-license HLS synthesis/simulation from generated-RTL compilation requiring a valid Vivado licence. Publicly documented commercial tooling can still impose reproduction friction. [Vitis platform and licensing](https://www.amd.com/en/products/software/adaptive-socs-and-fpgas/vitis.html)

A promising spatial alternative is **IRON/MLIR-AIE for Ryzen AI NPUs**. The project exposes tile placement, data movement and vector compute through an open MLIR compiler toolchain, with a corresponding LLVM AI Engine target. Its documented Python examples support compile, run and verification workflows; host setup requires supported NPU hardware and drivers. AMD also lists IRON and Triton-XDNA among its research open-source projects. This suggests a bridge from packet-processing hardware to accelerator compiler work. However, this investigation has not established an exact device, public compile-only workflow, or authoritative tile/local-memory feasibility oracle. Keep it as a follow-up research candidate, not an equally validated alternative to IREE. A Ryzen NPU flow must not be conflated with a complete open FPGA implementation flow. [MLIR-AIE](https://github.com/Xilinx/mlir-aie), [AMD research projects](https://www.amd.com/en/corporate/research/open-source.html)

## Decision implications and remaining uncertainty

The next investigation should select **one NVIDIA GPU/compiler boundary**, checking public installation, authentic resource constraints, baseline strength and measurable escalation value. IREE's NVIDIA coverage must be verified independently of its AMD tuning examples. Keep eBPF as a comparison. For future spatial mapping research, determine whether one named MLIR-AIE device offers public compile-time tile, local-memory and routing checks reproducible without hardware. Career relevance informs preference; it does not replace experimental validity.

The user is near Bristol and interested in NVIDIA. Hardware budget and exact preferred role remain unspecified. No Bristol-specific compiler vacancy was verified; UK-remote compiler and Bristol-listed performance roles are distinct evidence. The strongest portfolio outcome would be a reproducible result, explicit negative findings, and a small contribution maintainers find useful, even if the LLM tier adds little value.
